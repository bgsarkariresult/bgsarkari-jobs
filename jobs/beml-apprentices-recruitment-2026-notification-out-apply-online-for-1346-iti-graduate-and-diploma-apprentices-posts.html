BEML Apprentices Recruitment 2026 Notification Out - Apply Online for 1346 ITI, Graduate and Diploma Apprentices Posts

Summary: Complete information for BEML Apprentices Recruitment 2026 Notification Out - Apply Online for 1346 ITI, Graduate and Diploma Apprentices Posts. Check key details, selection process, and direct download links below.

Overview

BEML Limited, a Government of India Enterprise under the Ministry of Defence, has invited online applications for BEML Apprentice Recruitment 2026 under Advertisement No. L&D/01/2026, dated 18.08.2026. The company is engaging apprentices for a one-year apprenticeship duration under the Apprentices Act, 1961, across its manufacturing units at Bangalore, Mysore, KGF, and Palakkad Complexes, offering a total of 1346 training slots.

Important Dates

Event Date
Notification Release Date 18.08.2026

Online Application Closing Date 08.09.2026

Vacancy

BEML Limited has announced a total of 1346 training slots for apprenticeship engagement, distributed across four manufacturing complexes:

  • Bangalore Complex - 400 slots
  • Mysore Complex - 227 slots
  • KGF Complex - 608 slots
  • Palakkad Complex - 111 slots
