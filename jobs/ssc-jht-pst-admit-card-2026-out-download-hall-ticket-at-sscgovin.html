How to Download the SSC JHT PST Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official SSC Eastern Region website, sscer.org.
  2. Notice ya link dhundhein jo "Physical Standard Test (PST) for the post of Sub-Inspector (Hindi Translator) in CRPF" se related hai.
  3. Admit card / call letter download link par click karein jab yeh 7th August 2026 ko active ho.
  4. Registration Number aur Password se login karein.
  5. Aapka PST admit card screen par aayega. Details ko dhyan se check karein.
  6. Download karein aur PST centre par le jaane ke liye clear printout nikaalein.

Details Mentioned on the Admit Card

Admit card par aam taur par yeh details hoti hain:

  • Candidate's name, roll number, and registration number
  • PST date, reporting time, and venue address
  • Candidate's photograph and signature
  • Category and post applied for
  • General instructions for the test day

Admit card download karne ke baad har detail verify karein aur koi discrepancy hone par SSC Eastern Region office ko turant report karein.

Documents Required at the PST Centre

  • Printed copy of the PST admit card
  • A valid original photo ID proof
