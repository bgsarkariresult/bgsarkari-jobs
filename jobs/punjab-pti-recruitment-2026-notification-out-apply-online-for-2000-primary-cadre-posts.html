Punjab PTI Recruitment 2026 Notification Out

The Education Recruitment Directorate, Punjab (ERD Punjab) has released a recruitment notification for 2000 Physical Training Instructor (PTI) posts under the Primary Cadre in the School Education Department, Punjab. This recruitment is based on memo no. 1/2026869727/1 dated 19-06-2026 issued by the Director of School Education (Elementary), Punjab.

ERD Punjab PTI Recruitment 2026 Overview

Particulars Details
Organization Education Recruitment Directorate, Punjab (ERD Punjab)
Department School Education Department (Elementary Education), Punjab
Post Name Physical Training Instructor (PTI), Primary Cadre
Total Vacancies 2000
Vacancies Reserved for Women 650
Governing Rules Punjab State Elementary Education (Physical Training Instructor) Service Rules, 2023
Application Mode Online
Official Website erd.punjab.gov.in
Notification Date 31-07-2026

Important Dates

Event Date
Online Application Start Date 01-08-2026
Online Application Last Date 31-08-2026
Last Date for Fee Payment 31-08-2026

Who Can Apply?

  • Candidates with 10+2 qualification.
  • At least a two-year diploma or certificate in Physical Education (D.P.Ed/C.P.Ed or equivalent).
  • Must meet the prescribed age limit.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website: erd.punjab.gov.in.
  2. Fill out the online application form.
  3. Submit required documents.
  4. Pay the application fee.
  5. Submit the application before 31-08-2026.

Stay updated for further announcements regarding the written test, physical eligibility test (PET), or result declaration on erd.punjab.gov.in.
