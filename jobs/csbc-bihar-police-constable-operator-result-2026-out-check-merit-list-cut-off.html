CSBC Bihar Police Constable Operator Result 2026 Out

The Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC) has officially declared the written examination result for the post of Constable (Operator/Prachalak) in the Bihar Police Radio & Technical Non-Gazetted Cadre. The result, released on 18.08.2026, covers 993 vacancies advertised under Advertisement No. 02/2026. Candidates who have qualified are now shortlisted for the next stage of selection, the Physical Efficiency Test (PET).

How to Check CSBC Bihar Police Constable Operator Result 2026

  1. Visit the official CSBC website: csbc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Look for the notice titled "Results: Written Examination for the post of Constable (Operator) in Bihar Police (Advt. No. 02/2026)" on the homepage.
  3. Click the link to open or download the official result PDF.
  4. Open the downloaded PDF and use Ctrl + F on desktop (or the search icon on mobile PDF viewers).
  5. Enter your roll number to check whether you have qualified for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET).
  6. Save or print the result page for future reference.
