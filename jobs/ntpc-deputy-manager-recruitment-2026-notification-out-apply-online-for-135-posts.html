NTPC Deputy Manager Recruitment 2026 Overview

Particulars Details
Organisation NTPC Limited (A Govt. of India Enterprise)
Advertisement No. 10/26
Posts Advertised Deputy Manager (Electrical), Deputy Manager (Mechanical), Deputy Manager (C&I) – all E-4 Grade
Total Posts 135 (40 + 55 + 40)
Minimum Qualification B.E./B.Tech in relevant discipline with 60% marks
Experience Required Minimum 10 years in Power Sector
Upper Age Limit 40 years
Pay Scale Rs. 70,000 – 2,00,000 per month
Application Mode Online only
Application Fee Rs. 500/- for General/EWS/OBC; no fee for SC/ST/PwBD/XSM/female candidates
Selection Process Shortlisting, Written Test, Personal Interview
Job Location Any NTPC Station/Project/JV/Subsidiary

Important Dates

Event Date
Commencement of Online Application 12 August 2026
Last Date for Online Application 26 August 2026

Vacancy

  • Electrical: 40 Posts
  • Mechanical: 55 Posts
  • C&I: 40 Posts

Eligibility

Full-time B.E./B.Tech degree in the relevant discipline with at least 60% marks from a recognised University/Institution.

Pay Scale

E-4 Grade/IDA, Rs. 70,000 – 2,00,000 per month (w.e.f. 01.01.2017).

Application Fee

  • Rs. 500/- for General/EWS/OBC candidates
  • No fee for SC/ST/PwBD/XSM and female candidates

Age Limit

Upper age limit is 40 years for all three posts.

Selection Process

Selection will be based on shortlisting, written/computer-based test, and personal interview or a combination thereof, as per NTPC's discretion.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official NTPC careers website: www.ntpc.co.in.
  2. Fill in the online application form.
  3. Upload required documents.
  4. Pay the application fee (if applicable).
  5. Submit the application before the last date.

Important Instructions

  • Ensure all details are correct before submitting the application.
  • Keep a copy of the application form for future reference.
