IOCL Marketing Division NR Apprentice Recruitment 2026

IndianOil Corporation Limited (IOCL), Marketing Division, has announced the recruitment for Technician, Graduate, and Trade Apprentices under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Total 433 apprentice seats are available across various locations in the Northern Region.

Overview

Particulars Details
Organisation IndianOil Corporation Limited (IOCL), Marketing Division, Northern Region
Advertisement Number IOCL/MKTG/NR/APPR/2026-27/1
Total Apprentice Seats 433 (across 9 States/UTs)
Application Mode Online, through NAPS/NATS portal + Microsoft Office Form
Application Start Date 07 August 2026, 10:00 AM
Application Last Date 06 September 2026, 5:00 PM
Selection Mode Merit list based on qualifying marks; no written test/interview
Official Website www.iocl.com/apprenticeships

Vacancy Details

IOCL has notified a total of 433 provisional apprentice seats across 9 states/UTs in the Northern Region. The seats are categorized as follows:

  • Technician Apprentice (Discipline Codes 101–106)
  • Trade Apprentice/ITI (Discipline Codes 107–111)
  • Graduate Apprentice (Discipline Code 112)
  • Trade Apprentice-Data Entry Operator (Fresher & Skill Certificate Holders) (Discipline Codes 113–114)

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must have:

  • Class X pass with relevant ITI trade certification
  • A recognised Diploma in Engineering
  • A graduate degree in any discipline

Age Limit

Age limit will be considered as of 31 August 2026.

Stipend

Details regarding stipend will be provided upon selection.

Important Dates

  • Application Start Date: 07 August 2026
  • Application Last Date: 06 September 2026

Selection Process

Selection is purely merit-based on qualifying marks, with no written test or interview.

Required Documents

  • Educational Certificates
  • ID Proof
  • Passport Size Photo

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official website: www.iocl.com/apprenticeships
  2. Select the appropriate portal (NAPS/NATS).
  3. Fill in the application form.
  4. Submit the form before the last date.
