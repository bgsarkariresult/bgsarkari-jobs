About HPSC PGT Computer Science Result-II 2026

This Result-II is a continuation of the result HPSC first declared on 12 August 2026 for the PGT Computer Science posts under Advt. No. 23/2026. The Commission has now released an additional list of candidates recommended for appointment, in ascending order of roll number, separately for the ROH Cadre and the Mewat Cadre. The recommendation is issued by the Deputy Secretary, HPSC, Panchkula, and is dated 21 August 2026.
