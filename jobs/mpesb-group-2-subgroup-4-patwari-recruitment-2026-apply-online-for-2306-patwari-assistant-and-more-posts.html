MPESB Group 2 Subgroup 4 Patwari Recruitment 2026

Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), Bhopal, has released the notification for the Group-02 Subgroup-04 Combined Recruitment Exam 2026, offering a total of 2,306 vacancies.

Overview

Particulars Details
Conducting Body Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), Bhopal
Exam Name Group-02 Subgroup-04 Combined Recruitment Exam 2026
Total Vacancies 2,306 (200 Patwari-Divyang + 2,106 other posts)
Application Mode Online only, via esb.mponline.gov.in

Important Dates

Event Date
Application Start Date 04.08.2026
Application Last Date 18.08.2026
Correction Window 04.08.2026 to 23.08.2026
Exam Date 22.09.2026 onwards

Application Fee

Category Fee
Unreserved Candidates ₹500
MP-domicile SC/ST/OBC/EWS/Divyang Candidates ₹250

Vacancy Criteria

  • 200 Patwari posts reserved for Divyang candidates under the Land Resource Management Department, Gwalior.
  • 2,106 vacancies for various other posts including Assistant Grade-3, Computer Operator, Surveyor, Revenue Inspector, and more.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit: 18–45 years for Patwari (Divyang) posts and 18–40 years (up to 45 with relaxation) for other Group-2 Subgroup-4 posts.

Exam Pattern

The written examination will be conducted in two shifts at various exam centers.

Selection Process

Selection will be based on the performance in the written examination.

How to Apply

Eligible candidates can apply online from the official website esb.mponline.gov.in.

Important Links

For detailed information, candidates are advised to download the official notification PDF from the MPESB website before applying.
