RRB Group D City Intimation Slip 2026 Out - Download Link CEN 09/2025 Exam City Details

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released the RRB Group D City Intimation Slip 2026 on 29th July 2026 for candidates appearing in the Computer-Based Test (CBT) on 8th August 2026, under CEN 09/2025. The slip is being issued in a staggered, exam-date-wise manner at the candidate login portal, rrb.digialm.com. Candidates need their Registration Number and Date of Birth/Password to log in and check their allotted exam city, date, and shift. This slip is not the final admit card; it only informs candidates of their exam city in advance so they can plan travel.
