How to Download UPSC Civil Services IAS Mains Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official UPSC website at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Go to the "What's New" or "Examinations" section on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link titled "e-Admit Card: Civil Services (Main) Examination, 2026."
  4. You will be redirected to the UPSC e-Admit Card portal (upsconline.nic.in).
  5. Enter your Registration ID or Roll Number and Date of Birth as required.
  6. Complete the security check/CAPTCHA and submit the details.
