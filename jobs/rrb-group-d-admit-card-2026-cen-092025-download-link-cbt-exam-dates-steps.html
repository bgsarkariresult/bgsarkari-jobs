RRB Group D Admit Card 2026 (CEN 09/2025) - Download Link, CBT Exam Dates & Steps

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has started releasing the RRB Group D Admit Card 2026 for the Level-1 (Group D) Computer Based Test under CEN 09/2025. The admit card is being issued in phases, roughly four days before each candidate's scheduled exam date, on the official RRB candidate login portal. Candidates can download their hall ticket using their Registration Number and Date of Birth or Password.

How to Download RRB Group D Admit Card 2026

  • Visit the official website of your respective Regional Railway Recruitment Board, or the candidate login portal at rrb.digialm.com / rrbcdg.gov.in.
  • Click on the link titled "RRB Group-D CBT-I Admit Card 2026 (CEN 09/2025)."
  • Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth or Password.
  • Complete the security check (captcha), if prompted.
  • Click Submit or Login.
  • Your admit card will appear on the screen; check the exam date, shift timing, reporting time, and exam centre address carefully.
  • Download the PDF and verify every detail against your application.
  • Take at least two clear printouts for the exam day and for your records.
