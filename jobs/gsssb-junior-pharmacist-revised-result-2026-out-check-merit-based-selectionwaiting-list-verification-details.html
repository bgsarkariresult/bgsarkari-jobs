GSSSB Junior Pharmacist Revised Result 2026 Out

The Gujarat Subordinate Service Selection Board (GSSSB), Gandhinagar, has released a revised list on 06/08/2026 under G.No. 329/202526, naming prospective candidates who are eligible for certificate verification for the post of Junior Pharmacist, Class-3. This list has been prepared for inclusion in the selection list and waiting list, based on the merit of marks obtained by candidates in the competitive examination. This article covers what the list means, key dates, and how to check it on the official GSSSB website.

Documents Required for Verification

While GSSSB has not published a document checklist specific to this revised list, candidates called for Junior Pharmacist certificate verification are generally expected to keep the following originals and photocopies ready:

  • Educational certificates: B.Pharm / Pharm.D degree, or D.Pharm diploma with experience certificates (as applicable)
  • Gujarat State Pharmacy Council registration certificate
  • Date of birth proof (school leaving certificate/mark sheet)
  • Caste/category certificate, if claiming reservation
  • Disability certificate, if applicable
  • Application confirmation number and admit card
  • Recent passport-size photographs and a valid photo ID

This is a general guide based on the recruitment's known eligibility norms, not an official checklist for this specific list. Candidates must confirm the exact required documents from the official notice.
