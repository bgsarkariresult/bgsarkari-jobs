SSB PET/PST Physical Admit Card 2026 Out

The Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) has released the admit card for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) for candidates who applied for Constable to Sub Inspector (Non-GD) posts. The admit card was released on 08 August 2026, and the physical test will begin from 19 August 2026. Candidates can download their hall ticket from the official website, recruitment.ssb.gov.in, using their registration ID and password.

How to Download SSB PET PST Admit Card 2026

  1. Visit the official SSB recruitment website: recruitment.ssb.gov.in.
  2. Look for the link titled "E-Admit Card for the Post of Constable to Sub-Inspector (Non-GD)-2025, for PET and PST."
  3. Open the notice; it will contain the direct hall ticket portal link.
  4. On the admit card portal, enter your registration ID and password.
  5. Submit your details to view and download your SSB Non-GD Physical Admit Card 2026.
  6. Take a clear A4-size printout of the admit card to carry to the exam centre.

Important Instructions for SSB Physical Test 2026

  • Carry a printed copy of the admit card along with a valid original photo ID proof to the exam centre.
  • Reach the designated centre well before the reporting time mentioned on the hall ticket.
  • Follow the specific list of items to carry and items not to carry as printed on your individual admit card.
  • Keep your registration ID and password safe for future stages of recruitment.
  • Regularly check recruitment.ssb.gov.in for updates or notices.
