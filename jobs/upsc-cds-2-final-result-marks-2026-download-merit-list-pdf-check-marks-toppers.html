How to Check UPSC CDS 2 2025 Result

  1. Visit the official UPSC website at upsc.gov.in.
  2. Click on the "Written Results" section under the "Examinations" tab.
  3. Select "Combined Defence Services Examination (II), 2025 – Final Result."
  4. Open the PDF file for your respective academy (IMA, INA, Air Force, or OTA).
  5. Use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac) to search your roll number or name.
  6. Download and save the PDF for future reference, such as document verification.
